Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about listening faster without losing comprehension.

Does changing speed distort the narrator's pitch? expand_more
No. Modern audiobook and podcast apps (Audible, Spotify, Apple Books, Libby) use pitch correction algorithms. When you speed up the recording, the pitch stays natural rather than sounding high-pitched or "chipmunk-like."
What is the ideal listening speed for comprehension and retention? expand_more
Cognitive studies show that speeds between 1.25x and 1.5x provide virtually identical recall rates compared to 1.0x for conversational English narration. Above 1.75x, comprehension slightly drops unless you build up tolerance gradually over several chapters.
Is this calculator free or does it require an account? expand_more
AudioSpeed is 100% free with no paywall, no credit card, and zero registration required. Bookmark this page whenever you need quick book pacing or listening calculations.
Which apps allow custom playback speeds? expand_more
Virtually every leading audiobook service supports custom speed settings:
  • Audible: 0.5x to 3.5x in 0.05x or 0.1x increments
  • Spotify: 0.5x to 3.5x increments
  • Libby / OverDrive: 0.6x to 3.0x custom speeds
  • Apple Books & Storytel: Variable stepping dials
